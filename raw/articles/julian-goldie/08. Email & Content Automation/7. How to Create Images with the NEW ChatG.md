# 7. How to Create Images with the NEW ChatG

https://youtu.be/OWVvi8QE0Qc
Step 1: Access the Image Creation Tool
Open 
ChatGPT 4.0
.
Click on the 
three dots (…)
 in the top right corner of the chat window.
Select 
“Create Image”
 from the dropdown menu.
✅
 You’ll see a blue “Create image” tag show up. That means it's working!
Step 2: Upload or Screenshot Your Reference Image
Take a screenshot or upload an image (like a YouTube thumbnail, Facebook ad, or banner).
Make sure it’s okay to use (watch out for copyright stuff!).
Paste or upload the image into ChatGPT.
Step 3: Give Clear Instructions
Be specific with your edits.
Example:
“Make the text red and change the background to deep sea blue.”
🎯
 You can ask for style changes, layout updates, text edits—whatever you need.
Step 4: Let ChatGPT Generate the Image
It’ll take a few moments.
While waiting, you can upload or test other designs.
Step 5: Review the Output
ChatGPT will generate a new version based on your request.
Example:
Old thumbnail said “The End” — new one says “The End of Designers” with updated icons and styles.
Step 6: Make More Edits (Even Specific Parts!)
You can 
select parts of the image
 and make additional changes.
Example:
“Make this text green” or “Change this icon to a robot.”
Step 7: Download or Continue Editing
Once happy, download the image.
Or keep tweaking until it's just right!
