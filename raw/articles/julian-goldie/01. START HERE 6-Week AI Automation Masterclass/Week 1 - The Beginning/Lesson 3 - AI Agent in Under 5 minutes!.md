# Lesson 3 - AI Agent in Under 5 minutes!

In the third lesson of the 6-Week AI Automation Masterclass, Vick goes over how to create your own AI Agent in under 5 minutes.
To be quite clear, what we create in this lesson is a CustomGPT. We intentionally opted to teach you something so simple yet so impactful.
With CustomGPT's you can have an array of assistants for a wide span of tasks. From Email writers to Transcript Analyzers to Personal Assistants, Health Coaches etc.
In this lesson, we impose a new challenge: Create your own CustomGPT and share it with the group.
A lot of people fail to realize you can monetize your CustomGPT's. You can add instructions to it so that every time it pumps out an answer it shares your landing pages, products, services etc etc.
Share your CustomGPT's with the community in a post → the one we like the most, we'll make sure to pin so that you can get feedback + grow your personal brand!
