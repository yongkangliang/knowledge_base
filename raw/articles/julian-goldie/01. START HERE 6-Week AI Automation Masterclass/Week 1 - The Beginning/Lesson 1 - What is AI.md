# Lesson 1 - What is AI

In the first lesson of this 6-Week AI Automation Masterclass Vick covers the fundamentals - what is AI.
We talk about the history of AI, the beginnings, where can you find AI in everyday life and how people use AI without even knowing.
This lesson is intended to educate you on AI and its application in order to reduce the fear and friction when you encounter or you decide to use AI.
The more knowledge you acquire on AI the less you'll be against using it.
