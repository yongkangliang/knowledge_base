# Lesson 1 - Have a business mindset for automation!

Homework Assignment: AI Automation Business Mindset
Primary Tasks:
1. Conduct a Task Audit
Identify and document 3 repetitive or low-value tasks in your department
Focus on tasks that consume significant time or resources but deliver minimal strategic value
Document: What the task is, how long it takes, how often it's done, and why it's currently manual
2. Share With Your Team
Bring your findings to your next team meeting
Discuss the potential impact of automating these identified tasks
Gather additional insights and suggestions from colleagues
Ask team members if they've identified similar automation opportunities
3. Build Your Business Case
Estimate time and resources currently spent on these tasks
Calculate the potential ROI if they were automated
Document both tangible benefits (time saved, cost reduction) and intangible benefits (employee satisfaction, error reduction, consistency)
Challenge Assignment:
Sketch Your Automation Roadmap
Create a 90-day implementation plan for your first automation project
Include:
Specific processes to target
Tools to explore and evaluate
Success metrics and KPIs
Timeline and milestones
Commitment Component:
Set Implementation Timeline
Choose a specific date to present your automation proposal to decision-makers
Commit to beginning your first pilot project
Share your timeline with the community for accountability
This homework moves you from theoretical understanding to practical application, giving you concrete next steps to begin your AI automation journey in your specific business context.
