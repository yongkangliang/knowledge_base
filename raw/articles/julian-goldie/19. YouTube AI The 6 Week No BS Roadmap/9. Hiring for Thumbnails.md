# 9. Hiring for Thumbnails

Hiring for Thumbnails
How I Outsourced YouTube Thumbnails in 24 Hours:
...And Get Thumbnails with a 8.8% CTR
It was finally time to hire a YouTube thumbnail creator!
WHY:
Easy to outsource!
Saves 100s of hours over time
Makes YouTube more scalable
Avoids creative burnout
It's a low value task
PROCESS:
Posted a job ad for free to UpWork
Job Post Example
:
 https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yzdWsGZU6sWR86uScVrS9JCLtTf1JmD1fWSOCq30Ya8/edit?usp=sharing
Filter out anyone who hasn't been hired before. doesn't have $1,000+ earnings on UpWork or isn't a specialist at thumbnails
This filtered down to 3 people
Then I sent this message to get the free sample:
Hey! Please can you create a free sample for my channel here:
 https://www.youtube.com/@JulianGoldieSEO/videos
 Pics of me here:
 https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sSi1LepAdjTBtCO0KofpqJkLZm8THLZ8?usp=sharing
2 designers came back to me: 1 had great thumbnails
Now I get 2 thumbnails a day designed and split test them daily to check what gets higher CTR
Instructions are very simple + minimal
e.g. Create a thumbnail for a video I'm making about jason barnard - The video is about How to Rank #1 with EEAT
RESULTS:
8.8% CTR
Thumbnails created in hours
Price = $5 per thumbnail
Saves a LOT of time!
Got a top performing video yesterday after hiring him
