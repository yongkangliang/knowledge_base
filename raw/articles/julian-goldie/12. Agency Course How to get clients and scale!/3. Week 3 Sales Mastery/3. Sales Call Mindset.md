# 3. Sales Call Mindset

https://youtu.be/UH6hA2_2ejs
Ensure you're ready 5 minutes before your call by doing the following:
- Find a quiet space where you won't be disturbed.
- Start recording the call.
- Review the survey questions listed in the calendar invite to see their website and budget.
- Shift your focus from hoping for a "YES" to providing an accurate assessment.
- Clear your mind of any stress or distractions from earlier in the day or previous calls, and stay focused.
- Use your Google Meet link from before
GUIDE ON CONDUCTING SALES CALLS:
- Begin with casual conversation for the first 5 minutes to create a connection.
- Assist them in determining the number of backlinks needed for their desired keywords (refer to my previous call examples).
- Use 
https://app.ahrefs.com/keywords-explorer
 for help.
- Walk them through the Sales proposal you’ve created
- Be ready to address any questions they bring up.
- Conclude by inquiring about their timeline for getting started.
