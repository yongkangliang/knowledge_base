Example Questions You 
can
 Ask Yourself to Test How Good Your Offer/Product Is:
Is the service offering clearly defined and easy to understand?
Does the customer instantly grasp what they will receive?
Is there a fixed price for the service?
Can customers easily understand the cost without needing a custom quote?
Does the service solve a specific problem or meet a specific need for your target audience?
Is the service relevant and valuable to your intended customers?
Is the delivery process standardized and repeatable?
Can the service be delivered consistently to multiple customers without significant customization?
Can the service be scaled without a proportional increase in resources or time?
Is it feasible to serve more customers with the same level of quality without increasing workload linearly?
Are the outcomes or benefits of the service tangible and measurable?
Can customers easily see and measure the value they will receive?
Is the service differentiated from competitors?
Does it offer unique features or benefits that set it apart in the market?
Is the service easily marketable and understandable in your marketing materials?
Can the service be effectively communicated in marketing campaigns?
Does the service offering include clear terms and conditions?
Are the scope, limitations, and expectations clearly set out for the customer?
Have you received positive feedback or interest from your target market about the service?
Is there evidence that your target market sees the value and is interested in the offering?
