No hypothetical hires: 
Hire when it hurts and when you’re burnt out or running out of time to do the task.
Figure out where you’re spending your time: 
Typically this boils down to 80/20. 80% of your time spent on 20% of the work. So track your time for a week > Now you know what’s costing you the most time
Use TinyStopwatch on Mac or just Google stopwatch
After the time audit:
Figure out the highest ROI tasks
Eliminate the tasks you don’t need
Outsource the tasks have a high ROI and someone else can handle
Even if you don’t hire, this is a VERY important task:
Because you know where you’re spending your time
And what you need to stop doing/eliminate
Focus more about what to stop VS what to do:
E.g. time how long it takes to create a blog post > Then beat that time at the same level of quality = High leverage gain
This compounds over time
E.g. I was doing 100 things for our agency BUT not growing:
Only when I eliminated all the other time suckers that we really grew fast
Just because you can do something doesn’t mean you’re obligated to do it
ACTION ITEMS:
For at least 2 weeks, audit your time
Look at what you're spending most of your time on: What do you need to eliminate? What do you need to delegate?
