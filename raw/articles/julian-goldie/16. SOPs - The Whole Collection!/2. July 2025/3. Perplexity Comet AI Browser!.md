Overview
Comet is a next-gen AI-powered browser by Perplexity that acts like a 24/7 research assistant. It finds, analyzes, and summarizes info for you—saving you hours and giving you deeper insights than traditional search engines.
Step-by-Step Action Plan
1. Download & Set Up Comet Browser
Visit the Perplexity website and download the Comet browser.
Import your Chrome bookmarks and extensions (Comet is built on Chromium, so everything transfers easily).
2. Start Your First Research
Open Comet and enter your research question
 in the search bar (e.g., “Best marketing strategies for local businesses”).
Instead of just showing a list of links, Comet will:
Read dozens/hundreds of sources.
Pull the most relevant and recent information.
Summarize key findings, trends, and strategies.
Show sources so you can verify info.
3. Use Comet’s Research Features
A. Comet Assistant
Summarize all open tabs instantly.
Close duplicate tabs with one click.
Find previously read articles (even from last week).
Schedule meetings based on your emails.
B. Deep Research
For bigger topics, ask Comet to do a “deep dive.”
Comet will analyze multiple sources, check for patterns, cross-check facts, and create a detailed report in minutes (what used to take a human hours or days!).
4. Leverage Contextual Awareness
Comet remembers your research history.
If you researched “local marketing” last week and now ask about “social media ads,” Comet will connect the dots and provide related strategies.
5. Stay Private
Set your data/privacy settings—Comet gives you full control.
Use built-in ad blocking and tracker protection.
6. Who Should Use This?
Anyone who needs to find and analyze info fast: marketers, writers, students, consultants, lawyers, doctors, business owners, or just everyday life (vacation planning, shopping, etc.).
7. Maximize Results
For business, use Comet for:
Competitor analysis
Market research
Content research and creation
Deep-dive business reports
For personal tasks:
Plan trips and get activity/price comparisons
Research major purchases (e.g., cars, gadgets)
Create itineraries and compare deals
8. Human Oversight is Key
Always double-check important facts and insights.
Use your own judgment, especially for high-stakes decisions.
Think of Comet as a “super-assistant,” not a replacement for your expertise!
Pro Tips
Use natural language—Comet understands plain English and can connect ideas from previous sessions.
Use Comet’s assistant to manage your browser and research chaos (summarize tabs, close duplicates, etc.).
Experiment with “deep research” for big projects—get comprehensive overviews fast.
