Step 1: Understand the Opportunity
Claude (by Anthropic) just released a feature that lets anyone build and share AI-powered apps—no coding required!
These aren’t just simple chatbots. These are interactive, adaptable, and sharable apps—hosted for you, for free.
You don’t pay for other people’s usage; users sign in with their own Claude account.
Step 2: Pick a Real-World Problem
Think about something in your business or daily life that’s repetitive, time-consuming, or annoying.
Examples: Creating invoices, managing content, sorting customer feedback, planning social posts, etc.
Ask yourself: What would save me an hour a week or more?
Step 3: Describe Your App to Claude
Open Claude in a browser tab.
In plain English, 
describe exactly what you want your app to do.
The more detail, the better! (See prompt examples below.)
Step 4: Example Prompts (Copy & Use!)
1. Invoice Generator App
Create an interactive invoice generator app with these features:
Business info section (company name, address, phone, email, logo upload)
Autogenerated invoice number, date picker for invoice date and due date
Client information (client name, company, address, email; save for reuse)
Invoice items: add/remove line items, description, quantity, rate, total
Subtotal, tax %, tax amount, final total
Payment terms dropdown (Net30, due on receipt), custom notes
Modern blue/white color scheme, helpful placeholder text in every field
2. Content Repurposing App
Build a content repurposing app that transforms a blog post into:
Multiple social media formats (3 LinkedIn, 3 Twitter, 2 Facebook, 2 Instagram captions, with hashtags)
Email content (subject line options, opening paragraphs, newsletter sections, CTA suggestions)
Video scripts (60-second script, 3-minute outline, 30s TikTok script)
Easy copy buttons, character/word counters, tabs for each output, and a clean organized layout
Step 5: Let Claude Build It
Paste your prompt into Claude.
Wait 1-3 minutes for Claude to generate and build your app.
Review and test the app in the artifacts window.
Make tweaks by describing changes to Claude.
Step 6: Share & Use
Share the app’s link with your team, clients, or customers.
Anyone with a Claude account can use it (their usage counts against their plan, not yours).
You can build unlimited apps—no extra cost.
Step 7: Think Bigger
The real opportunity isn’t just making fun tools—it’s 
solving real problems
 for your business and clients.
Think: What can you automate, speed up, or improve?
Could you sell access, offer this as a service, or scale your own business faster?
Step 8: Experiment!
Don’t overthink it. Start with something simple.
The people who experiment the most with AI, win.
Try different prompts, app ideas, and features—iterate fast.
Step 9: Stay Ahead
This is an early-mover advantage. Most people don’t know this exists yet.
The faster you start learning and building, the more ahead you’ll be as this tech goes mainstream.
