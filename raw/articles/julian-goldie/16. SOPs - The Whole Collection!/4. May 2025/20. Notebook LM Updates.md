1. Set Up Your Language Preferences
Go to 
Notebook LM
 at 
notebooklm.com
.
Click on 
Settings
 (top-right).
Find 
Output Language
.
Select your preferred language (supports 50+ languages).
Click 
Save
.
2. Upload Your Learning Resources
You can upload:
YouTube videos (just paste the link).
Google Docs, Slides, PDFs.
Audio files or text snippets.
📌
 You can upload up to 
300 sources
 — super helpful for deep research or training.
3. Generate Audio Overviews
Go to 
Create New
 → Upload a source.
Choose 
Deep Dive Conversation
.
This starts a chat between two AI hosts that simplify complex content.
✅
 Example: Tell the hosts to "Explain this like I'm in 3rd grade" or "Focus on practical use cases."
4. Train Your AI Assistant
Upload videos, documents, or content your team needs to learn.
Ask questions based on that content (e.g., “How does Quen 3 compare to other models?”).
Notebook LM will respond using your uploaded material.
5. Use Interactive Mode
Hit 
Play
 on an audio overview.
Ask follow-up questions live — just like chatting with AI hosts.
6. Generate Learning Materials
With one click, turn your content into:
✅
 Study Guides
✅
 Mind Maps
✅
 FAQs
✅
 Briefing Docs
✅
 Timelines
🎯
 Great for onboarding, self-paced learning, or building internal training libraries.
7. Share With Your Team
Click 
Share
.
Add their emails (like Google Docs).
They’ll get access and can learn from your curated content.
💡
 Bonus: Use analytics to see who's engaging with your shared materials.
