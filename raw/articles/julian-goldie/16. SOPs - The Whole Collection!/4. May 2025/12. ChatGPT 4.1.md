 Overview
GPT-4.1 is more literal, more obedient, and more capable. You’ll get the best results by:
Giving super clear instructions.
Breaking complex tasks into steps.
Guiding it like a high-performing team member.
This is your cheat sheet for how to prompt GPT-4.1 inside ChatGPT — not via API, no code required.
1. Agentic Prompts 
🤖
Train GPT-4.1 to act like an assistant that owns the task from start to finish.
✅
 What to Say in Chat:
You are my assistant. Keep solving until the task is fully complete. Don’t stop until you’re sure it’s done.
If you’re not sure about something, ask or explain what you need. Don’t guess.
Think before each step. Reflect after. Keep going until the problem is solved.
❌
 What Not to Say:
"Be helpful."
Too vague. GPT-4.1 will only do what you clearly ask.
2. Step-by-Step Thinking 
🧩
Chain-of-thought = better output.
✅
 What to Say in Chat:
Think through this step by step before giving your answer.
First, plan. Then solve. Then explain what you did.
Bonus Tip:
If you want deeper thinking, say:
After each step, explain your logic and what you’re doing next.
❌
 What Not to Say:
“Just tell me the answer.” You’ll miss the magic of GPT-4.1’s deeper reasoning.
3. Handling Long Prompts or Documents 
📜
Use GPT-4.1’s long memory like a pro.
✅
 What to Say:
Only use the context I provided. If it’s not in there, say you don’t know.
Ignore anything irrelevant. Focus only on what matters to the question.
Extra Tip:
Put your 
question at the end
 of long inputs so it doesn’t get buried.
4. Precise Instruction Following 
📋
GPT-4.1 takes your words literally. Treat it like a team member that needs detailed SOPs.
✅
 What to Say:
Follow these exact rules:
Greet the user.
Ask a clarifying question.
Answer only if you're sure.
Speak casually. Use short paragraphs. Never use dashes.
Add These Too:
“Don’t repeat the same phrase more than once.”
“Avoid fluff. Be direct.”
❌
 What Not to Assume:
GPT-4.1 no longer fills in the gaps like older models. Spell out everything.
5. Prompt Formatting Tips 
🧱
Structure = clarity. GPT-4.1 performs better with format.
✅
 Use This Layout:
# Role
You are an SEO expert.
# Objective
Create a plan to grow organic traffic.
# Instructions
- Keep it under 300 words
- Use bullet points
- Include a call-to-action at the end
# Tone
Direct, clear, friendly
Pro Tip:
Start every chat by stating the role, goal, and rules. It’s like setting the tone for the conversation.
6. Fixing Prompt Failures 
🧯
🔍
 If It’s Not Working:
Are your instructions too vague?
Did you forget to say what not to do?
Are your expectations realistic?
✅
 Fixes:
Add an example.
Add a sentence like: "Avoid over-explaining."
Clarify with: “If unsure, ask me first.”
⚠
️ Common Problems:
Stops early? → Say: "Don't stop until you finish the task."
Repeats? → Say: "Don't reuse the same phrase twice."
Off-topic? → Say: "Only respond based on the info above."
✅
