 Step-by-Step SOP: Using Perplexity Labs
Step 1: Accessing Perplexity Labs
Go to 
Perplexity.ai
Ensure you are on the 
Pro Plan
 (~$20/month).
Click on the 
Labs
 tab from the homepage.
Step 2: Choose Your Project Type
Perplexity Labs works best for:
Deep market research
Trading strategies & performance dashboards
Creative writing (e.g. sci-fi film scripts)
Visual storytelling & storyboard generation
Data visualization with real-time charts
Custom dashboards for global economic tracking
Pick one use case or idea you want to build.
Step 3: Run Your First Project
Use natural language prompts. For example:
“Create a deep research report on AI updates in 2025.”
“Build a trading strategy dashboard using recent stock data.”
“Create a noir-style sci-fi film concept with 9 storyboards.”
Labs will:
Search the web
Pull in latest data
Auto-generate assets (charts, storyboards, presentations)
Show sources, citations, & task breakdown
Step 4: Explore and Edit
Review the 
dashboard
 or 
report
.
Click into 
Assets
 to download charts, images, CSVs.
Use the 
Sources
 tab to verify references.
Check the 
Tasks
 section for transparency in how it was built.
Step 5: Compare with Other Tools (Optional)
You can compare Perplexity Labs output to:
ChatGPT
Google Search
Other tools like Manis or GenSpark
Perplexity is faster and more visual—especially good for storytelling, dashboards, and research-heavy tasks.
Step 6: Browse the Project Gallery
Go to the 
Project Gallery
 inside Labs.
Find inspiration from what others are creating.
Click into projects, download assets, reuse structures.
Step 7: Customize the AI Model
Inside Preferences > Pro Mode:
Choose models like Claude, DeepSeek R1, or Sonnet.
Labs uses multiple models + agents automatically for complex tasks.
Step 8: Embed, Share or Present
Click “Share” to send dashboards or visual reports.
Use links for team access or embed in Notion, Google Docs, etc.
All assets are reusable and downloadable.
