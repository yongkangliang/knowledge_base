“Build a tipping calculator to help calculate tips on a bill with friends. Use a pink and purple color scheme.”

Add styling or features (optional)

 Step 5: Generate the App
Paste your prompt into Firebase Studio
Click 
“Prototype This App”
Wait for the tool to create your blueprint and code
