 STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS
🎯
 1. Set Up Notebook LM
Go to 
https://notebooklm.google.com
Log in with your Google account
Click 
Create New Notebook
🎥
 2. Add a Source (e.g. YouTube Video)
Copy a YouTube video URL
In Notebook LM, click “
Add Source
”
Paste the video URL and click “Insert”
💡
 
This trains the AI on that video — like creating your own custom GPT.
🌍
 3. Generate Content in Multiple Languages
Change the 
Output Language
 to your desired one (e.g. Spanish, Afrikaans)
Click “
Generate
”
You’ll get AI podcasts, study guides, FAQs, timelines, mind maps — in any language!
🎙
️ 4. Create an AI Podcast
After uploading your source, click "Generate Podcast"
The AI will synthesize the content into a podcast
You can:
Play it
Speed it up
Share or download it
Even make it 
interactive
 so you can talk to the AI mid-podcast!
🧠
 5. Enter Interactive Mode
Click “Interactive Mode”
Join the AI-generated podcast and 
speak
 with it in real-time
Ask questions about the content and it will respond intelligently
💾
 6. Import Multiple Sources for Custom GPT
Use the “Discover” button to find other relevant sources
Click “Import” to bring them into the notebook
Ask questions based on 
multiple sources
 and the AI will pull context-rich answers
📝
 7. Generate Prompts, Notes & Study Guides
Ask: “Based on the sources, create some awesome prompts for me.”
Get pre-made:
Study guides
Timelines
Briefing documents
Mind maps
FAQs
👥
 8. Train Your Team with Notebook LM
Add internal SOPs, training docs, or Google Docs as sources
Click “Share” → Set permissions (view notebook or just chat)
Your team can now 
ask questions
 and get instant answers, trained on YOUR materials
🧠
 Example prompt:
"Tell me about the Sales Manifesto" → AI replies using your sales training SOP
🧪
 9. Access Gemini API (Optional)
Go to: 
https://aistudio.google.com
Click "Get API Key" → Use this in tools like 
OpenRouter
Recommended model: 
Gemini 2.5 Flash
📅
 10. Use It for Weekly AI News Roundups
Add your latest video links into one notebook
Ask: “Create a quick roundup of this week’s AI updates.”
Boom – instant summary
🤖
 11. Customize Chat Style
Go to “Chat Settings”
Set custom personality like:
"You're a sales trainer for Goldie Agency."
The AI will speak like a real trainer!
