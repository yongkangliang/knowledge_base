 Step-by-Step SOP:
🟢
 1. Access Qwen Coder
Go to Qwen Coder and select the 
"WebDev"
 option
Use either 
Qwen Max
 or newer models like 
Qwen 3
🟢
 2. Create Your App
Inside the WebDev interface, enter your prompt.
Example Prompt:
"Create a basic SEO cost calculator app"
🟢
 3. Wait for the App to Generate
Qwen will start coding your app immediately
You’ll see the app UI being built with live functionality
🟢
 4. Test the App
Use the form fields (e.g., enter “10 keywords”) to test app functionality
Check if values like “total cost” update correctly
🟢
 5. Deploy the App
Click on the 
“Deploy”
 button
Choose:
Public
 (anyone with the link can access)
Private
 (only you can access)
Your app will now be live on a 
Qwen subdomain
 (no login required for users)
🟢
 6. Share Your App
Copy the public link and share it via:
Twitter
Your website
Email lists
Example usage:
“Check out this free SEO cost calculator I built in 3 mins!”
