 Overview
This SOP shows you how to use 
Perplexity Pages
, a little-known SEO loophole that allows you to publish AI-generated pages on a high-authority domain (
DR 80+
) and rank on Google quickly—even without backlinks.
🔧
 Step-by-Step SOP
1. Set Up a Perplexity Pro Account
Go to 
Perplexity.ai
Upgrade to a Pro account (required to create Pages)
2. Find a Keyword to Target
Use tools like:
Ahrefs free keyword tool
Ubersuggest
Or even 
Perplexity
 itself:
Example Prompt:
“Give me 20 local SEO keywords for 'SEO Training' based on wealthy cities”
Filter for
:
KD (Keyword Difficulty) under 10
Volume over 100
3. Search the Keyword in Perplexity
Example: Type 
"SEO Training Birmingham"
 into Perplexity
Let it generate a search result
Hit 
“Convert to Page”
4. Edit & Optimize the Page
Edit the title
 to include your exact keyword (e.g. "Best SEO Training in Birmingham")
Insert a section
 about your product or service:
Example:
Title: "Why is Julian Goldie's SEO Elite Circle the Best?"
Add content like a mini sales page
Add media
 (images, videos, links)
Customize the source context
 to include your business name and sales page URL
5. Publish the Page
Click 
“Publish”
Your URL will look like: https://www.perplexity.ai/page/your-title-here
6. Get It Indexed
Since indexing can be tricky, try:
Sharing it on social media
Linking to it from other high-traffic or branded pages
Using tools like 
IndexInject
 or 
IndexMeNow
7. Multiply the Strategy
Don’t rely on just one page. Create 10–20 pages targeting low-competition, long-tail keywords.
Example niches:
Local SEO (“Plumbers in San Antonio”)
Amazon products (“Best bird feeder for small birds”)
Affiliate keywords (“Top 10 patio grills under $300”)
8. Quality Control
Edit before publishing—don’t just push AI spam
Run it through 
Julian's SEO Quality Control Checklist
Ensure the content is:
Useful
Easy to read
Human-friendly
✅
 Bonus Tips
NoFollow Links
: Perplexity links are nofollow—don’t expect backlink value, but it can drive real traffic.
Local SEO
: Works great for location-based keywords.
Resume Pages
: You can even create personal/professional pages like “Who is [Your Name]?” to build authority.
