 Pro Tips for Better Results:
Be specific
: Instead of "make it better," say "increase energy with faster cuts and upbeat music"
Chain commands
: Combine multiple edits in one prompt for efficiency
Reference styles
: "Edit this like a MrBeast video" or "Make it feel like a TED talk"
Include technical specs
: "Export in 4K 60fps with HDR color grading"
Set constraints
: "Keep it under 60 seconds" or "Make sure the logo is visible throughout"
