# 14. Get GenSpark to answer your emails.

Get GenSpark to answer your emails.
13:37
📩
 SOP: How to Get GenSpark to Answer Your Emails Automatically
Tool by Julian Goldie
Purpose
: Automate email replies, manage calendar events, and summarize your inbox using GenSpark’s AI Super Agent.
✅
 Step-by-Step Instructions:
🧠
 1. Set Up GenSpark Email + Calendar Integration
Goal
: Connect your Gmail + Google Calendar with GenSpark
Steps
:
Log into GenSpark.
Go to the “Tools” section.
Click 
“Add Tool”
 → Choose Gmail and Google Calendar.
Authorize the integration when prompted.
You’re now ready to automate email handling + scheduling.
✉
️ 2. Summarize Your Inbox + Prioritize Tasks
Prompt to Use in GenSpark
:
“Summarize today’s emails. Highlight urgent ones. Don’t show private info.”
What Happens
:
GenSpark pulls emails from Gmail.
It identifies key messages needing attention.
It categorizes spam, business opps, follow-ups, etc.
Gives you a daily summary and 
actionable suggestions
.
🔥
 
Pro Tip
: You can also export the summary to Notion with one click!
🖊
️ 3. Automatically Draft Email Responses
Prompt Example
:
“Write a draft response to the AMCast collaboration email, selling the benefits of buying a sponsored video with Julian Goldie SEO YouTube Channel.”
What It Does
:
Auto-writes a full email response.
Includes custom benefits and urgency.
You can edit and format the email.
Choose to 
save as draft
 or 
send it live
 via GenSpark.
📬
 4. Send Yourself Automated Emails
Use Case
: Email yourself a reminder or newsletter.
Prompt Example
:
“Email Julian at 
julian@domain.com
 about the new GenSpark features. Make sure it’s nicely formatted.”
✅
 GenSpark creates a clean, promotional-style email with icons, formatting, and headings.
🔁
 5. Automate Follow-Ups to Leads
Prompt Idea
:
“Look through my emails. Find people I haven’t followed up with in 7 days. Draft a follow-up email for each and send it to my inbox.”
Perfect For
:
Sales reps
Agency owners
Follow-up automation at scale
📅
 6. Use Voice or Text to Create Calendar Events
Prompt Example
:
“Create a calendar event for tomorrow to research the latest AI updates.”
🎯
 What Happens:
It adds the event to your Google Calendar.
Includes details, agenda, and areas to focus on.
Schedules instantly with perfect formatting.
