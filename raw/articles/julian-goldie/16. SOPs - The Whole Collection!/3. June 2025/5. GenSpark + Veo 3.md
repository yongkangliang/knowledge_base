 STEP 1: Come Up with a Concept

 STEP 2: Write a Prompt
Paste into GenSpark:
text
CopyEdit
Use Veo 3. Generate a series of short 8-second cinematic video clips for a surreal comedy vlog. Character: Bigfoot Scene: Forest workout Style: Vlog, funny, vibrant visuals Include: Camera movement, lighting, pacing, short dialogue Request: Give prompt ideas before generating scenes
✅
 Tip: Request approval before video creation to save GenSpark credits.
🎥
 STEP 3: Generate Scenes
Ask GenSpark to generate scene-by-scene.
Review and approve each one before moving on.
Be specific: “Add characters in the background,” “Include selfie stick,” “More surreal humor.”
🗣
 STEP 4: Keep Dialogue Short
Since Veo 3 clips are 8 seconds max, keep the dialogue tight.
📌
 Example: “What’s up forest fam? Bigfoot here, time to get swole.”
🧵
 STEP 5: Stitch Scenes Together
Once your scenes are generated:
Ask GenSpark: “Stitch these scenes into one complete video.”
Or use NA10 or ffmpeg for better control.

text
