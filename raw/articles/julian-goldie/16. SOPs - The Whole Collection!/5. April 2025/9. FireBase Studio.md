Firebase Studio is Google’s new 
AI-powered app builder
.
Think of it like Project IDX reborn — but way better.
Competes with platforms like Bolt or Lovable.
Totally free, no coding required.
Step 2: How to Get Access
Visit: 
studio.firebase.google.com
Sign in using your 
Gmail account
.
Boom—you’re in! 
🎉
Step 3: Start Building Your First App
Enter your idea into the prompt box (e.g., “SEO flashcard app”).
Click 
“Prototype with AI”
.
Firebase Studio builds the blueprint, layout, colors, and even transitions—automatically!
Step 4: Customize Your App
You can:
Change app name
Use your brand colors
Modify the layout
If something breaks? Hit 
“Fix Error”
—it auto-corrects issues like magic 
🪄
Step 5: Publish and Share Your App
Click 
“Publish”
.
Firebase gives you a 
hosted subdomain
 link.
You can:
Share the link
Add teammates by email
Generate a QR code for access
Step 6: Use Advanced Features
Switch to code view
 to tweak your app manually.
Version control
: Restore earlier versions any time.
Drawing tool
 and other fun UI tweaks available.
It also integrates with 
Gemini 2.5 Flash
 for more advanced features.

Post your own builds and get feedback.
