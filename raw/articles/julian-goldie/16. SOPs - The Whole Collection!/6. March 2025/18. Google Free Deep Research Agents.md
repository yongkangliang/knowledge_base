SOP: How to Use Google Gemini's Deep Research AI Agent
Overview
Google Gemini's Deep Research AI is a powerful tool that goes beyond typical AI-generated responses. It mimics the work of a PhD-level research assistant, gathers up-to-date web data, and generates in-depth reports.
Step-by-Step Guide to Using Google Gemini Deep Research
Step 1: Access the Gemini Deep Research AI
Go to 
gemini.google.com
In the 
top left menu
, select 
Deep Research
This feature is 
completely free
 and available in 
45 languages
 across 
150 countries
Step 2: Set Up Your Research Task
Define your research question. Example:
"What are the best T-shirts with sleeves in the UK for 2025?"
Customize your research:
Edit the 
Research Plan
 before submitting
Specify 
region, industry, or competitors
Click 
Start Research
Step 3: Let Gemini Do the Work
AI Research Process:
Breaks down the problem
Searches 
expert sources
 on the internet
Analyzes data and generates a report
Provides a 
list of cited sources
Wait time:
 ~
5-10 minutes
Step 4: Review the Research Report
Click on 
"Show Thinking"
 to see the reasoning process
Click on 
"Sites Browse"
 to view all 
sources used
Gemini 
prioritizes reputable sites
 like 
Amazon, Uniqlo, Men's Health, The Independent
Avoids unreliable sources (e.g., 
Medium.com
)
Once the research is complete, review the 
full-length report
Step 5: Export & Share the Report
Click 
Export to Google Docs
Open the document and review the 
formatted
 research (includes citations & tables)
Share with your team:
Click 
Share
 in Google Docs
Set sharing permissions to 
Anyone with the link
Copy & send the link
Step 6: Summarize & Simplify (Optional)
The full report may be 
too long or academic
To make it more readable:
Copy the report
Paste it into 
Gemini AI
Prompt: 
"Summarize this in first-grade level language, add headings, and make it easy to understand."
Use the 
simplified version
 for quick insights
