Example Use Cases:
1. Turn a Google Doc into a YouTube-ready video in one click
Upload any Google Doc, SOP, or article. Instantly generate a full animated video with voiceover that you can post directly to YouTube.
2. Convert a YouTube video into a podcast episode
Paste a YouTube URL. Generate a natural-sounding two-person AI conversation summarizing and discussing the content. Perfect for repurposing.
3. Summarize a huge PDF into a 2-minute video
Upload a 50+ page PDF (
ebook
, report, training guide) and watch it condense into a short, engaging video you can share.
4. Create a multilingual version of your content
Generate Spanish, French, or Mandarin audio and video overviews from your English material. Show switching the output language in settings.
5. Build a “personal AI” trained on your business
Feed Notebook LM 
your
 About page, case studies, and blog posts. Then ask it to create marketing content with your tone and CTAs built in.
6. Rank on Google with repurposed content
Take a video, generate a written blog post version targeting a keyword, and show how you could post it to your site to capture SEO traffic.
7. Turn a webinar into 5 content formats
Upload a recorded webinar and instantly create: a video summary, a podcast, a blog post, social media snippets, and an FAQ sheet.
8. Build an instant “interactive trainer”
Upload your team’s training playbook. Show how new hires can ask questions and get instant answers from Notebook LM instead of waiting on you.
9. Discover sources automatically for deeper content
Type a topic (e.g., “AI SEO tools”). Add the discovered sources with one click. Generate a well-researched video overview without doing manual research.
10. Create educational micro-lessons
Upload a textbook chapter. Generate short, actionable video lessons with quizzes and homework that you can sell or give away.
