SOP: How to Use Google Notebook LM Video Overviews to Learn, Teach & Scale Knowledge
Purpose
To quickly transform any document into a professional, AI-narrated video that makes complex information easy to understand, customize for different audiences, and share effectively.
Step 1 – Access Notebook LM
Go to 
Notebook LM
 on desktop (English only for now).
Ensure you have the latest version — this feature is rolling out now and is free for all users.
Step 2 – Upload Your Content
Accepted formats: 
PDF
, Word docs, research papers, meeting notes, business plans, technical reports, policy papers, scripts, or any text-based content.
Upload the document to your Notebook.
Step 3 – Create a Video Overview
Click 
Video Overview
 in the top panel.
Wait for AI to process your file — this only takes a few minutes.
The AI will:
Create narrated slides with visuals, images, diagrams, and quotes pulled directly from your content.
Highlight data points and key ideas.
Present in a professional, easy-to-understand way.
Step 4 – Customize for Your Audience
Tell AI your needs before generation:
Example: “Explain this for beginners” or “Focus only on the financial analysis.”
Specify your audience: Students, executives, investors, industry peers.
Set your 
learning goals
.
AI tailors the tone, focus, and complexity accordingly.
Step 5 – Use Playback Controls
Standard video controls: pause, rewind, skip, change speed.
Compare 
Video Overview
 vs 
Audio Overview
 to see which works better for comprehension.
Step 6 – Leverage New Studio Panel Features
Create 
multiple outputs
 from the same document:
Several video overviews for different audiences.
Multiple audio overviews in different languages.
Mind maps and study guides — all stored in one organized place.
Multitask:
Watch a video while viewing a study guide.
Listen to audio while navigating a mind map.
Step 7 – Export & Share
Download videos as 
MP4
.
Share via public links.
Use for:
Client presentations
Team onboarding
Investor pitches
Education & training
Step 8 – Apply in Your Business
Replace long, unread manuals with short explainer videos.
Turn dense industry research into shareable, understandable content.
Shorten onboarding time from 
weeks to days
.
Teach complex processes faster.
Step 9 – Keep an Eye on Updates
Google plans more video formats beyond narrated slides.
Mobile & more languages are coming soon.
Early adoption = advantage before competitors catch on.
Pro Tip:
 The more specific you are with AI instructions, the better the output. Treat it like briefing a real designer or video editor.
