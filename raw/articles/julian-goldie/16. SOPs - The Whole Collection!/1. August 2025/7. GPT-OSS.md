 Core Overview
Open-source
 large language models released by OpenAI.
Available under Apache 2.0 license.
Two sizes: 
gpt-oss-120B
 (116.8B params) and 
gpt-oss-20B
 (20.9B params).
Designed for 
reasoning, tool use
, coding, and multilingual understanding.
Can run on consumer-grade hardware thanks to 
quantized weights (MXFP4)
.
🏗
️ Architecture & Training
Based on 
Mixture-of-Experts
 (
MoE
) transformer design.
Uses 
FlashAttention
, 
Rotary Position Embeddings
, and 
Grouped Query Attention
.
Context window: up to 
131,072 tokens
 with 
YaRN
.
Trained on a 
STEM-heavy, code-rich
 text-only dataset.
Safety filters
 applied during pretraining to block hazardous data.
🧪
 Performance Benchmarks
Reasoning
: Very strong on math (AIME), above average on factual tasks (GPQA).
Coding
: Near o4-mini levels on 
Codeforces
 and SWE-bench.
Tool Use
: Supports web browsing, Python, and custom dev functions via Harmony format.
Health
: 120B model nearly matches OpenAI o3 and beats GPT-4o on 
HealthBench
.
Multilingual
: Strong performance across 14 languages (MMMLU), near o4-mini.
Self-improvement tasks
: Trails behind OpenAI o3/o4-mini.
🧠
 Reasoning Capabilities
Supports 
three reasoning modes
: low, medium, high.
Higher mode = longer chains of thought (
CoT
) and better accuracy, at higher cost.
Scales well with reasoning effort—users can trade off latency for accuracy.
🛠
️ Post-training Enhancements
Uses Harmony Chat Format (with system, developer, and user roles).
Includes 
structured outputs
, function calls, and interleaved tool use.
Personality and alignment similar to ChatGPT via RL-based training.
🔐
 Safety & Security
Aligned to OpenAI safety standards by default.
Trained with 
deliberative alignment
 and 
instruction hierarchy
.
Evaluated for:
Disallowed content
Jailbreak resistance
Prompt injection protection
Does not reach "High Risk" capability on bio or cyber tasks, even after adversarial fine-tuning.
⚠
️ Limitations & Cautions
More hallucinations
 than o4-mini (especially in factual QA).
Instruction hierarchy adherence
 is weaker than o4-mini.
CoT
 may include 
unfiltered or hallucinated content
. Developers must moderate.
Developers can fine-tune models—but also bear responsibility for safety guardrails.
📊
 Head-to-Head Comparison
120B
 matches or beats o4-mini on coding and reasoning, and approaches o3 on health.
20B
 punches above its size—competitive with o3-mini in many benchmarks.
Both outperform many older open-weight models (like 
DeepSeek
 R1 or early 
Qwen
).
